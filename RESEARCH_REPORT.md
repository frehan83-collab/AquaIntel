## 11. EMERGING TECHNOLOGY & DISRUPTION TRACKING

### RAS (Land-Based) — Reality Check
- **3.5M tonnes planned capacity on paper**, but <1% of current production actually comes from RAS
- Atlantic Sapphire: 5,096t harvested 2025, expects positive EBITDA only by end of 2026
- Salmon Evolution: 4,403t in 2025, Phase 2 → 18,000t, Phase 3 → 36,000t target
- Nordic Aquafarms Belfast ($500M) **abandoned** Jan 2025 — regulatory risk is real
- RAS **NOT cost-competitive** with cage farming — relies on premium pricing
- **Realistic 2030 estimate: 150-250K tonnes (5-8% of global supply)**

### Offshore Farming
- SalMar Ocean Farm 2 approved Jan 2025 (70m high, 120m diameter, 3-4M fish)
- Arctic Offshore Farming approval **withdrawn** over welfare violations
- SalMar exploring site east of Shetland (Scotland)
- Material impact: 10-20K tonnes by 2030

### Genetics
- **AquaBounty (GMO salmon) shut down completely** — GMO is dead commercially
- CRISPR sterile salmon: 2028-2030 earliest regulatory approval in Norway
- CRISPR lice resistance: 2030-2035+ if technically feasible (transformational)
- Genomic selection: already commercial and delivering measurable genetic gain

### Cell-Cultured & Plant-Based
- **Wildtype got FDA approval mid-2025** — first cell-cultivated seafood cleared for US sale
- Restaurant-scale only; NOT a supply threat this decade
- 5 US states have banned cell-cultured meat sales
- Plant-based salmon: $1.3B market growing to $3.4B by 2035, but not competitive with fresh fillets

### Compliance Events
- **EU mandatory digital traceability: January 2026** — all catch/lot data must flow digitally
- This is a compliance event creating immediate demand for traceability tools

### Climate Risk
- Under current emissions: -3% production by 2050, -14% by 2090
- Iceland: **54,789 tonnes in 2024 (record), 35% CAGR since 2016** — benefiting from warming
- First Water raising EUR 265M for 60,000 MT/year land-based in Iceland
- Southern production zones (Chile coast, Scotland, southern Norway) face mounting pressure

### FishIntel Tracking Features
1. **RAS Production Tracker** — actual vs planned capacity for all listed RAS companies
2. **Climate Risk Score** per production region (SST-integrated)
3. **EU Traceability Compliance Tools** — January 2026 deadline
4. **Iceland Production Monitor** — fastest-growing origin, increasingly material
