# Role: Commercial Lead (salmon trading networks)

**Type:** full-time, remote-friendly (Oslo/Trondheim or Bergen preferred)
**Reports to:** founder
**Compensation:** base + uncapped commission tied to **paid pilots converted**, plus
an options package. No performance-fee revenue (deferred — see
`core/legal/performance_fee.py`).

## Why this role exists

The product is built and the money path works end to end. What is missing is
distribution into the people who actually trade, buy and plan salmon: **desks,
processors, integrated producers and insurers**. That requires someone who already
has those relationships and can hold a technical conversation about biomass
momentum, lice pressure and basis.

## What you will do

1. **Recruit 5–8 paid 90-day design partners** (target: 3 desks, 2 processors,
   2 producers NO/CL, 1 insurer) using `docs/DESIGN_PARTNERS.md` as the runbook.
2. **Run the pilot lifecycle in the product**, not in a spreadsheet: create the
   partner, agree success metrics, onboard, record metrics
   (`/api/admin/partners/{id}/metrics`), drive to `converted` or `churned`.
3. **Own the pricing conversation** against the canonical catalog: Insight →
   Operator → Desk → Enterprise → Investor → Intelligence (+ licensed Data &
   Assurance). Do not invent tiers; do not discount quietly.
4. **Feed the product roadmap from the field**: the top three objections per ICP,
   weekly, in writing.
5. **Represent at AquaNor and NASF** using `docs/GTM_EVENTS.md`; book and run the
   meetings yourself.

## What success looks like (first two quarters)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Paid pilots signed | 5–8 |
| Pilots converted to annual contracts | ≥ 40% |
| Mean days from first call to signed pilot | ≤ 30 |
| ARR at end of Q2 | ≥ NOK 1.5M |
| Named reference customers usable in sales | ≥ 2 |

## What you need

- **Real salmon-industry network**: desks, processors, producers or feed/insurance.
  You can get a first call answered inside a week.
- Comfort with the numbers: NOK/kg, harvest volumes, lice counts, quota headroom.
  You can read `/api/indices/ai-sti` and explain the difference between a leading
  indicator and a price forecast.
- **Discipline about honesty.** You will be selling a product whose differentiator
  is refusing to claim accuracy it has not measured. If you would rather round a
  MAPE up, this is the wrong company.
- Written English and Norwegian; ability to run a demo without an engineer
  (`docs/GTM_DEMO_SCRIPT.md`).

## What you get

- A product that already does what it says: working paywall, real plan catalog,
  auditable indices, published method, and a legal register that says what we may
  and may not do with each data source.
- Founder-level context and a direct line to engineering.
- No gatekeeping: you can change the pitch and the pilot terms as fast as you learn.

## First 30 days

1. Read `docs/DESIGN_PARTNERS.md`, `docs/GTM_DEMO_SCRIPT.md`,
   `docs/ADVICE_BOUNDARY.md`; run the demo yourself against a staging deployment.
2. Build the 60-account target list with ICP tags.
3. Run 15 discovery calls; write up the objections verbatim.
4. Close the first 2 pilots.
