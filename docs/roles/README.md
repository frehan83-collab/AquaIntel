# Open roles

Three roles, written to be used immediately (post them, or send them to a
recruiter). Each is blunt about what exists, what does not, and what the person
will own.

| Role | Why it exists | File |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial Lead (salmon trading networks) | The product and the money path work; distribution into desks, processors, producers and insurers does not exist yet. Owns 5–8 paid 90-day pilots. | [commercial-lead.md](commercial-lead.md) |
| Enterprise & Security Engineer (SSO + SOC 2) | Enterprise deals stall on identity and evidence: SSO is not implemented and SOC 2 has not started. Owns both, plus the production gaps in `docs/PRODUCTION_TRUTH.md`. | [enterprise-security-engineer.md](enterprise-security-engineer.md) |
| Product Designer (field & mobile) | The Operator plan is the recurring-revenue tier and it is a desktop dashboard. Owns the one-handed field loop and honest offline capture. | [product-designer-field.md](product-designer-field.md) |

## Shared context every candidate should read before a first call

1. `AGENTS.md` — the non-negotiable rules (honesty layer, security, provenance).
2. `docs/GTM_DEMO_SCRIPT.md` — what we show and the objections we expect.
3. `docs/GTM_SECURITY_ONE_PAGER.md` — including the "not in place yet" table.
4. `docs/DESIGN_PARTNERS.md` — how pilots actually run.

## The hiring filter

The differentiator is refusing to claim what we have not measured (a MAPE, a
certification, a partnership). Every role here is judged first on whether the
candidate is comfortable with that — it is the product, not a policy.
