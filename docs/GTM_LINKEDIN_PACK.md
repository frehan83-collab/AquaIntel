# LinkedIn content pack (domain experts + founder)

**Cadence:** 2–3 posts/week, one format per category. Written to be posted by a
*domain expert* (a salmon person), not by marketing — that is why it works.

## Format 1 — "The Number and the Caveat" (Mon)

> Sea lice pressure nationally: AI-LPI at **{value}** ({band}).
>
> Same index, all inputs transparent: {components and weights}.
> One source degraded this week, so the badge says so and the response names it.
>
> That's the point — an indicator you can audit, or no indicator at all.
> Method v{version}: {link}

## Format 2 — "What the data can't tell you" (Wed)

> Three things this week's salmon data does **not** say:
> 1. {x}
> 2. {y}
> 3. {z}
>
> We publish our own gaps: /api/data-freshness shows every source as fresh, stale,
> dead or synthetic-only. If a source is dead, the index degrades rather than
> quietly interpolating.

## Format 3 — "Field note" (Fri, producer voice)

> A lice treatment costs roughly {cost} per event at our sites, and the decision to
> treat early or hold is where the money is.
>
> What changed for us: the quota headroom view. Not a recommendation — just the
> biology and the quota in one place before the weekly meeting.
>
> (If you operate in NO and want the pilot terms, they're in our docs.)

## Posting rules

- **Never** post a number that is not on a page you can link to.
- Always link the method or the provenance, not just the chart.
- No performance claims on prices; no "should buy/sell" framing (see
  `docs/ADVICE_BOUNDARY.md`).
- If a follower asks for a personalised recommendation, answer: "we don't make
  recommendations — here's the indicator and its method."
- Tag the data source when it is public (Fiskeridir, SSB), and cite the table.
